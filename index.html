Dive Oxygen Toxicity Explorer

See how CNS and pulmonary oxygen toxicity build up during a dive and recover afterward.

Educational tool only — not for real dive planning. Assumes a square dive profile (instant descent/ascent, constant depth/PO2).
Indexes to show

Hover the chart to see values at a given time.

Each index is plotted as a % of a recommended limit.

  • CNS% — % of the NOAA 1991 single-dive time limit; recovers with a ~90 min half-time. Hoyt 2025
  • OTU (also UPTD) — the classic pulmonary dose; % of 300 OTU/day; no recovery model. REPEX (NOAA)
  • ESOT — newer pulmonary dose that includes recovery; % of 450/day, the limit for 7 consecutive diving days. DMAC 35
  • ROT — probability of detectable pulmonary toxicity, valid only near 1.3 ATA; not plotted as it has no recommended limit. Shykoff 2015
